# Under Scrutiny

**Play it:** https://lukataylo.github.io/under-scrutiny/

Under Scrutiny is a VAT inspection you can rehearse before a real one. Mr. Whitmore, a fictional inspector, works through eight real expense claims from a startup's books, one at a time. For each claim you choose a position:

- **Defend claim:** make your case in writing or out loud, and attach evidence.
- **Amend claim:** pick a correction from a short list, with the £ effect shown on each option.
- **Need context:** ask for the rule that applies.

He reacts in character: suspicious, reading, pressing, stern or pleased. At the end you get findings: the VAT to correct, the penalty if you fix it yourself compared with the penalty if an inspector finds it, and a list of corrections for your accountant. The rehearsal makes one point: being honest early is cheaper than being caught.

Built at the Seapoint FinOps Hackathon, London, 20 Oct 2026, on data from the Seapoint sandbox.

## How it works

- One static page (`index.html`) and eight character poses (`character/`). There's no backend.
- Voice: Mr. Whitmore speaks through the browser's text-to-speech. **Speak instead** uses the browser's speech recognition (best in Chrome; allow the microphone).
- On this page, built-in rules judge your answers. In the claude.ai version, Claude plays the inspector and judges free-text defences, and only facts persuade him.
- The page is generated by a Claude Code skill (`under-scrutiny`). The skill pulls claims from the Seapoint MCP, picks the ones an inspector would pick, and builds the page.

## Disclaimer

Independent training simulation. Mr. Whitmore is fictional, and this project is not affiliated with HM Revenue & Customs. Penalty figures are simplified from HMRC's published inaccuracy penalty ranges and are not tax advice.
